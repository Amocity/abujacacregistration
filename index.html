CAC REQUIREMENTS

CAC Registration Requirements

Requirements depend on the registration type. The list below summarizes common requirements from CAC guidance; CAC may request additional information or documents.

Business Name

  • Proposed/approved business name
  • Proprietor details and address
  • Valid means of identification
  • Recent passport photograph
  • Proprietor signature
  • Phone number and email address
  • Other information requested by CAC

Limited Liability Company

  • Approved/reserved company name
  • Company type and principal business activity
  • Registered office address
  • Director/shareholder/PSC particulars
  • Valid identification and supporting documents as applicable
  • Company email and phone details
  • Applicable statutory documents/fees

Incorporated Trustee / NGO

  • Reserved organisation name
  • Constitution of the organisation
  • Trustee names, addresses and details
  • Valid identification for trustees
  • Passport photographs for trustees
  • Trustee declaration forms
  • Meeting minutes and newspaper publication where required
Important: Requirements can vary by entity and circumstances. We will confirm the current requirements before you submit documents. For official requirements and filing, CAC's current portal and guidance remain the authoritative source.

SCUML & TAX SUPPORT

SCUML & Tax Registration Support

These are separate compliance services and are not automatically required for every CAC registration.

SCUML Registration

SCUML registration applies to businesses and professions that fall within the categories regulated by the Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering (SCUML).

  • Requirements depend on the business category and entity type.
  • Common documents can include CAC registration documents and Tax Identification Number (TIN).
  • Additional sector-specific evidence may be required.
  • We can help identify the applicable SCUML requirements before submission.

Tax / TIN Support

We can help clients understand the tax-registration information needed after or alongside business registration.

  • Tax Identification Number (TIN) / taxpayer registration information
  • CAC registration number and business/company details
  • Business address, phone and email details
  • Nature of business and other tax-registration information

Tax Clearance / Certificate

A Tax Clearance Certificate (TCC) is different from a TIN. A TCC may be required for particular transactions or compliance purposes and is subject to the applicable tax authority's requirements.

  • We can provide guidance on the appropriate tax document for your situation.
  • We do not describe a TCC as a universal CAC-registration requirement.
  • Current tax-authority requirements should be confirmed before filing.
Important: SCUML is not a requirement for every business. Tax documents are also not one universal requirement for every CAC registration. Requirements depend on the entity, business activity and applicable regulatory rules.
