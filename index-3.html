Requirements

CAC Registration Requirements

Requirements vary by registration type. We help you identify the documents and information needed for your application.

Business Name

  • Proposed business name
  • Proprietor/owner details
  • Identification information
  • Business address and contact details
  • Other information required by the current CAC process

Company / Limited Liability

  • Proposed company name
  • Directors and shareholders information
  • Identification documents
  • Registered office information
  • Company details and supporting documents

Incorporated Trustee / NGO

  • Trustee information
  • Constitution and objectives
  • Meeting/minutes documentation where applicable
  • Required declarations and supporting documents

Compliance Support

  • SCUML support for eligible categories
  • Tax/TIN-related support
  • Tax clearance/compliance guidance where applicable
  • Requirements depend on the relevant authority and business type
